About Mana Miao 🐾

Mana Miao (MM) is a community-driven initiative to map, track, and care for stray and neighborhood cats. Our goal is to connect caretakers, promote TNR practices, and give every street cat a chance at a better life.

Built with 💛 by cat lovers, for cat lovers. Drop a pin, share a story, help a paw.

TNR Guide 💉

  • 1. Trap — Use humane box traps baited with wet food.
  • 2. Neuter — Take cats to a vet or TNR clinic for spay/neuter surgery.
  • 3. Return — After recovery, return cats to their original territory.
  • 4. Monitor — Continue feeding and tracking colony health.

Ear-tipped cats have already been neutered — no need to re-trap!
